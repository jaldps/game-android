# id `crowd` — "Mob Rush" (feel of Crowd City), portrait
Angled top-down camera that zooms out as your crowd grows. Drag to steer your leader; neutral grey/white pedestrians convert to your color on touch and run to join your swarm (color-pop + "+1" stream). Rival AI crowds of other colors; on collision the larger absorbs the smaller person-by-person (dramatic tug at the boundary); leaders get eliminated. 2:00 round, live leaderboard with counts, results with rank/medal/stars, coins, unlockable skins/colors, multiple cities. THOUSANDS of animated people (InstancedMesh, vertex-shader run cycle, per-instance color, flocking with spatial hash, tight packing so the swarm flows like liquid around corners). Rich city: streets w/ markings and crosswalks, sidewalks & curbs, plazas, parks, rooftops reachable via ramps/stairs, varied buildings w/ window textures, props (trees, benches, parked cars, fountains), soft shadows, AO, bright clean stylized palette.
